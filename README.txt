Jalapeños website — single-file version
========================================

Everything (colors, fonts, animations, the food photo) is packed
into ONE file: index.html

To see the page:
  Double-click index.html — it works right away, no unzipping needed.

To put it on GitHub:
  1. Go to github.com and sign in
  2. Click the + (top right) > New repository, name it e.g. jalapenos
  3. Click "uploading an existing file"
  4. Drag index.html in, then click Commit changes
  5. Go to Settings > Pages > Source: pick "main" branch, Save
  6. Wait a minute — your site is live at
     https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/jalapenos/
